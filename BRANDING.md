# Pecut AI — Brand & Trademark Guidelines

> **Summary:** The Pecut AI source code is released under the Apache-2.0 License. However, the Pecut AI name, logo, visual branding, and official domain names are protected trademarks and assets owned by the project maintainers. The open-source license applies to the code, not the brand.

---

## 1. Core Principle

We encourage you to use, study, fork, modify, and build upon the Pecut AI source code. At the same time, we must prevent user confusion between independent forks or derivatives and the official Pecut AI distribution / Pecut Cloud service.

Section 6 of the Apache License 2.0 explicitly states:
> *"This License does not grant permission to use the trade names, trademarks, service marks, or product names of the Licensor, except as required for reasonable and customary use in describing the origin of the Work and reproducing the content of the NOTICE file."*

---

## 2. Permitted Uses

You **MAY**:
- State truthfully that your project or product is *"Powered by Pecut AI"* or *"Based on Pecut AI Community Edition"*, provided that "Pecut AI" is not the most prominent part of the name.
- Link directly to the official repository (`https://github.com/pecut-ai/pecut-ai`).
- Distribute verbatim or modified source code under the Apache 2.0 license, while leaving original attribution and NOTICE files intact.
- Use the term "Pecut AI" in academic research, articles, tutorials, or conference talks discussing the project.

---

## 3. Restricted Uses

You **MAY NOT**:
- Name your fork, SaaS service, or commercial product simply *"Pecut AI"*, *"Pecut AI Official"*, or *"Pecut AI Pro"*.
- Use the official Pecut AI logo, icon, or trademarked visual assets as the primary branding of an independent service or modified fork without written authorization.
- Imply or state that your fork, plugin, or deployment is officially endorsed, maintained, sponsored, or certified by Pecut AI.
- Register domain names, social media handles, or trademark applications containing "Pecut AI" or confusingly similar variations.

---

## 4. Forking Best Practices

If you publish a modified version or host a public fork:
1. Choose a distinct, unique product name for your distribution (e.g., *"MyWorkspace (built on Pecut AI core)"*).
2. Replace or customize default branding elements (logo, application title, favicons).
3. Provide your own contact information, terms, and privacy policy for your users.

For brand licensing inquiries or partnership questions, please reach out to the project maintainers via GitHub Discussions or issues.
